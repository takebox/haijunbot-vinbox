# Summary Report Generation

## Research Goals

Integrate the research results of the previous 8 stages to generate a comprehensive, in-depth, and valuable open-source project research summary report, providing a final reference for technology selection and decision-making.

## Report Generation Steps You Must Perform

### Step 1: Collect and Organize the Results of Each Stage

**Documents that must be collected:**

- `01_project_background.md` (project background)
- `02_problem_solution.md` (problem and solution)
- `03_key_technologies.md` (key technologies)
- `04_architecture_design.md` (architecture design)
- `05_pros_cons.md` (strengths and weaknesses analysis)
- `06_competitor_analysis.md` (competitor comparison)
- `07_evolution_direction.md` (evolution direction)
- `08_followup_strategy.md` (follow-up strategy)

**Organization requirements:**

- Extract the core conclusions of each stage
- Identify key information and data
- Mark information sources and credibility
- Remove redundant and duplicate content

### Step 2: Write the Executive Summary

**Content that must be included:**

- A one-sentence introduction to the project
- Core strengths and highlights
- Main limitations and shortcomings
- Applicable scenarios and selection recommendations
- Follow-up and participation recommendations

**Summary requirements:**

- Concise and clear, kept to 300-500 words
- Highlight the key points, seize the essentials
- Objective and fair, avoiding bias
- Easy to quickly understand the project as a whole

### Step 3: Integrate the Project Overview

**Background information that must be integrated:**

- Basic project information (name, website, repository, license)
- The project's origin and development history
- Community ecosystem and maintenance status
- The project's positioning and core value

**Presentation methods:**

- Use tables to present basic information
- Use a timeline to present the development history
- Highlight the project's unique positioning

### Step 4: Summarize Technical Innovations and Solutions

**Core content that must be summarized:**

- The core problem the project solves
- The core solution adopted
- Key technical innovation points
- The rationale for technology selection

**Summary requirements:**

- Highlight technical innovation and value
- Explain the effectiveness of the solution
- Support with data and cases
- Avoid excessive technical detail

### Step 5: Summarize Architecture and Design

**Architecture content that must be summarized:**

- The overall system architecture
- Core components and their responsibilities
- Component interaction methods
- Important design patterns

**Presentation methods:**

- Use architecture diagrams to explain the overall design
- Use tables to present component responsibilities
- Highlight the design thinking

### Step 6: Comprehensively Analyze Strengths and Weaknesses

**Strengths that must be synthesized:**

- Technical strengths (performance, extensibility, etc.)
- Ecosystem strengths (community, support, etc.)
- Applicable scenario strengths
- Differentiated strengths compared to competitors

**Shortcomings that must be synthesized:**

- Technical limitations and shortcomings
- Ecosystem and community shortcomings
- Unsuitable scenarios
- Risks to be vigilant about

**Summary principles:**

- Objective and fair, seek truth from facts
- Distinguish strengths from applicable scenarios
- Provide concrete examples
- Avoid subjective assumptions

### Step 7: Provide Selection Recommendations

**Recommendations that must be provided:**

- Scenarios suitable for selection
- Scenarios not suitable for selection
- Comparison with the main competitors
- Concrete selection decision recommendations

**Recommendation framework:**

- Scenario 1: [description] -> Recommendation: [choose/not choose] -> Reason: [detailed explanation]
- Scenario 2: [description] -> Recommendation: [choose/not choose] -> Reason: [detailed explanation]
- ...

### Step 8: Provide Follow-up and Participation Recommendations

**Recommendations that must be provided:**

- How to continuously follow the project's development
- The recommended level of participation
- Considerations for application integration
- Risk management and response

**Recommendation content:**

- Concrete ways to follow up on information
- Entry points for community participation
- Best practices for application integration
- Risk identification and mitigation measures

### Step 9: Provide Decision Support

**Decision information that must be provided:**

- Project health assessment
- Technical maturity assessment
- Community activity assessment
- Risk level assessment

**Assessment dimensions:**

- Code quality
- Documentation completeness
- Community activity
- Security
- Maintenance stability

## Research Output Requirements

### Document Structure

**Document that must be produced:** `summary_report.md`

**The document must contain the following sections:**

1. Executive summary
2. Project overview
3. Summary of technical innovations and solutions
4. Summary of architecture and design
5. Comprehensive analysis of strengths and weaknesses
6. Selection recommendations
7. Follow-up and participation recommendations
8. Decision support
9. Reference list

### Content Quality Requirements

- **Must**: comprehensively integrate the research results of each stage
- **Must**: highlight the key points, avoid redundancy
- **Must**: provide clear decision recommendations
- **Must**: be objective and fair, with a basis
- **Must**: have a clear structure, easy to read
- **Forbidden**: simple stitching lacking integration
- **Forbidden**: subjective assumptions lacking basis

### Report Format Requirements

- **Must**: use markdown format
- **Must**: include visual elements such as tables and diagrams
- **Must**: provide a clear table of contents structure
- **Must**: mark all information sources

## Report Depth Check

After completing the report, you must confirm:

- [ ] It integrates the research results of all 8 stages
- [ ] It provides a clear executive summary
- [ ] It objectively summarizes the strengths and weaknesses
- [ ] It gives clear selection recommendations
- [ ] It provides practical follow-up recommendations
- [ ] The report structure is clear and easy to read
- [ ] All conclusions have sufficient basis

## Report Writing Techniques

### Information Integration Techniques

**Must master:**

- Extract the core, filter out secondary information
- Identify connections, build logical links
- Deduplicate and trim, avoid repetition
- Highlight the bright spots, seize the essentials

### Visualization Techniques

**Suggested to use:**

- Tables: compare information
- Lists: structure information
- Timelines: development history
- Scoring matrices: multi-dimensional assessment

### Decision Expression Techniques

**Must follow:**

- Clear and direct, no ambiguity
- Provide reasons, with a basis
- Consider scenarios, case by case
- Risk warnings, be responsible

## Common Mistakes

**❌ Wrong approaches:**

- Simply stitching together the documents of each stage
- Only listing results without analysis
- Lacking clear selection recommendations
- Subjective assumptions lacking basis

**✅ Correct approaches:**

- Deep integration, distilling value
- Comprehensive analysis, providing insight
- Give clear decision recommendations
- Objective and fair, with a basis

## Practical Tips

**Report generation tips:**

1. Write the executive summary first, then the body
2. Use comparison tables to clearly show differences
3. Support conclusions with data and cases
4. Provide concrete, actionable recommendations
5. Consider the needs of different readers
6. Review and update the report regularly
