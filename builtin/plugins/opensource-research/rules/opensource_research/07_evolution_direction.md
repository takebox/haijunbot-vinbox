# Evolution Direction Research

## Research Goals

Analyze the latest development trends, technological evolution trends, and future plans of an open-source project, predict the project's development direction, and provide forward-looking reference for long-term technical decisions.

## Research Steps You Must Perform

### Step 1: Research the Latest Development Trends

**Development trends that must be followed:**

- The latest version updates and feature releases
- Discussions on important issues and PRs
- Recent technical blog articles
- Important community events and news

**Keywords to search:**

- "[project name] latest updates"
- "[project name] new features"
- "[project name] changelog"
- "[project name] recent changes"

**Information sources:**

- The GitHub Release page
- The project's official website News/Blog
- Community Discussions
- Related coverage in technical media

### Step 2: Analyze the Technology Evolution Direction

**Evolution dimensions that must be researched:**

- Upgrades and migration of the tech stack
- Evolution and refactoring of the architecture
- Introduction and exploration of new technologies
- The direction of performance optimization

**Key research questions:**

- The project's development trend at the technical level
- What new technical challenges are being solved
- The plan for cleaning up technical debt
- The choice of future tech stack

**Keywords to search:**

- "[project name] future plans"
- "[project name] roadmap"
- "[project name] technical debt"
- "[project name] architecture evolution"

**Information sources:**

- The project's Roadmap document
- RFCs and design discussions
- Maintainers' technical blogs
- Technical discussions in the community

### Step 3: Research Community Planning

**Planning content that must be followed:**

- The official roadmap and milestones
- Community votes and proposals
- Short-term and long-term goals
- Prioritization

**Planning information to collect:**

- The plan for upcoming releases
- Core features of the next stage
- Long-term technical vision
- Improvements to community governance

**Keywords to search:**

- "[project name] roadmap"
- "[project name] future goals"
- "[project name] upcoming features"
- "[project name] milestones"

**Information sources:**

- The official Roadmap page
- The project's Planning Issues
- Maintainers' planning articles
- Community proposals and RFCs

### Step 4: Analyze the Impact of Industry Trends

**Industry trends that must be researched:**

- Development trends in related technical fields
- The evolution of industry standards and specifications
- Trends such as cloud-native and Serverless
- The impact of AI/ML on the project

**Key analysis content:**

- How industry trends affect the project
- Whether the project needs to adapt to new standards
- The technology evolution direction of competitors
- The opportunities and challenges brought by emerging technologies

**Keywords to search:**

- "[technical field] trends 2024"
- "[technical field] future"
- "[project name] industry trends"
- "best practices [technical field]"

**Information sources:**

- Technical industry reports
- Technical conferences and talks
- Trend analysis on technical blogs
- read_webpage to read industry articles

### Step 5: Research Maintainers' Views

**Views that must be understood:**

- Core maintainers' technical vision
- Their views on the project's future
- The differentiation direction from competitors
- Their understanding of user needs

**Information to search:**

- Maintainers' technical blogs
- Interviews and podcasts
- Conference talks and sharing
- Community AMAs (Ask Me Anything)

**Keywords to search:**

- "[maintainer name] interview"
- "[project name] vision"
- "[project name] future direction"
- "[maintainer name] talk"

**Information sources:**

- Maintainers' personal blogs
- Interviews in technical media
- Conference videos (YouTube, etc.)
- Community discussions

### Step 6: Predict Potential Risks and Challenges

**Risks that must be analyzed:**

- Technical risks (technological obsolescence, dependency issues)
- Community risks (maintainer attrition, community shrinkage)
- Competitive risks (the emergence of new competitors)
- Resource risks (funding, manpower shortages)

**Keywords to search:**

- "[project name] challenges"
- "[project name] risks"
- "[project name] maintenance concerns"
- "[project name] sustainability"

**Information sources:**

- Risk discussions in Issues
- Community concerns about the project
- Third-party risk assessments
- Analysis articles on technical blogs

### Step 7: Collect Changes in User Needs

**User needs that must be followed:**

- New needs raised by users
- Hot spots in feature requests
- User feedback on pain points
- Changes in market demand

**Information to search:**

- GitHub Feature Requests
- User use case discussions
- Community needs voting
- User feedback and suggestions

**Keywords to search:**

- "[project name] feature request"
- "[project name] user feedback"
- "[project name] needs"
- "help wanted [project name]"

**Information sources:**

- GitHub Issues and Discussions
- User forums and mailing lists
- Feedback on social media
- Surveys and interviews

### Step 8: Summarize the Evolution Direction

**Content that must be summarized:**

- The project's short-term development direction
- The project's long-term vision
- Key technical evolution points
- Potential risks and challenges

## Evolution Analysis Framework

### Short-term vs Long-term

**Must distinguish:**

- Short-term (6-12 months): clear plans and roadmap
- Medium-term (1-2 years): technology evolution and architecture improvements
- Long-term (3+ years): vision and strategic direction

### Driving Force Analysis

**Must analyze:**

- Driven by user needs
- Driven by technology trends
- Driven by competitive pressure
- Driven by maintainers' vision

### Risk Assessment

**Must assess:**

- The risk of technological obsolescence
- The risk of community shrinkage
- The risk of insufficient resources
- The risk of intensifying competition

## Common Mistakes

**❌ Wrong approaches:**

- Only looking at official publicity while ignoring actual progress
- Ignoring the impact of industry trends
- Not analyzing potential risks
- Treating speculation as fact

**✅ Correct approaches:**

- Analyze plans in light of actual progress
- Consider the impact of industry trends
- Comprehensively assess potential risks
- Distinguish between clear plans and reasonable speculation

## Practical Tips

**In-depth research strategy:**

1. Look at the project's roadmap to understand the official plan
2. Analyze recent issues to understand actual progress
3. Follow maintainers' blogs and talks to understand their vision
4. Read competitors' development trends to understand the landscape
5. Look at community discussions to understand user needs
6. Follow the latest sharing from technical conferences
